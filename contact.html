Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Custom PC build take?

Standard builds are completed and tested within 5-7 business days. For Forge Elite custom liquid loops, please allow up to 14 days for rigorous stress testing and leak checks.

Do you offer warranties on repaired consoles?

Yes! Every console repaired in our Medbay comes with a 90-day comprehensive warranty covering the specific parts replaced and the labor involved.

Can I track my gear during the repair process?

Absolutely. Once you hand over your hardware, you will receive a unique tracker ID. You can use it in our portal to see live updates (e.g., Diagnosing, Repairing, Ready).

How does the Trade-In program work?

Bring your old hardware to our store or ship it to us. Our technicians will evaluate it within 24 hours and offer you store credit or cash based on the current market value.
